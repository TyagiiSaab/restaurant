Our Story

Made with intention.

Ember & Oak started in 2018 with a secondhand oven, eight tables, and a simple idea — cook slow, source local, and give Panipat a room that feels like someone's home.

Chef Arjun Mehta works with five family farms within 60 km. Vegetables arrive each morning; bread is baked at noon; nothing is rushed. The room is low-lit and unhurried — built for birthdays, first dates, and Tuesday nights that turn into stories.

Our Story · In Full

Made with intention.

Ember & Oak began in 2018 with a secondhand oven and eight tables. Chef Arjun Mehta — raised in Panipat, trained in Delhi and Copenhagen — wanted a room that treated dinner as an evening, not a transaction.

Every morning, vegetables arrive from five family farms within 60 km. Bread is baked at noon. Stocks simmer all day. The oak oven is lit at 4 PM and never rushed. There are 85 seats, low light, and staff who know when to appear — and when to disappear.
